# M6c patched element-wise operations: TreeACI kernel on the Matsubara G0

## Status

Exploratory, not decision-grade. No paired protocol was declared in advance,
and up to eight single-thread processes ran side by side on the eight physical
cores of one host (sections 1 to 3 shared it with section 6 and B0), so
timings compare runs within one table only. The accuracy numbers are
deterministic for a given seed. Every table below is
generated by [`report.py`](m6c-elementwise/report.py) from the raw output in
[`m6c-elementwise/raw/`](m6c-elementwise/raw/), whose checksums are in
[`SHA256SUMS`](m6c-elementwise/SHA256SUMS).

Scope: `patched_elementwise` with the TreeACI kernel
(`TreeAciInterpolator`), step 5 of the
[M6c design record](../../docs/design/tree-patching-elementwise.md). The tree
RSI half is parked by the maintainer (2026-11-11): tree RSI has no in-run
error control, and its `rel_tol` is an LU pivot threshold, not an accuracy
setting.

Open: the comparison on gw-rs's low-temperature topology (`tt_nblock`,
T = 0.01, R = 12) waits for #784 (section 5); B0 was tried on one seed and
did not finish within its 2 h limit (section 6).

Not reported: a first round of runs on 2026-11-10/11 used the comb CTTN at
T = 0.01 and R = 12. That is the comb's worst regime for G0 (section 4), its
unpatched baseline cycled at the tolerance boundary (#784), and its raw data
was lost when the host crashed. Its patched-versus-unpatched timings are
withdrawn.

## Provenance

Each run group has a `PROVENANCE` file written by command: the SHA-256 of
the measured binary, the commit it was built from (`git rev-parse`), the
host and the thread environment. Every group except `784probe/bin-fix` uses
the same binary (SHA-256 `e232e17f…`), built in release from a clean
`feat/tree-patching-m6a-contraction` at `9dde54ca`. Harness:
[`benchmark_m6c_elementwise`](../rust/benchmark_m6c_elementwise.rs).

Host: AMD Ryzen 9 6900HX (8 cores, 16 logical CPUs), WSL2 kernel 6.18,
19 GB RAM. Each process is pinned to one even-numbered logical CPU with
`taskset` and runs with `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`. Timings exclude building the inputs and the
harness's accuracy check; a patched run's time includes the driver's own L2
measurement.

## Workloads and what is compared

Every workload is the gw-rs G0 setting: 2D Hubbard dispersion,
`mu = 0.5`, Matsubara frequencies `omega_n = 2 pi T (n - 2^R / 2 + 1/2)`,
`R` bits per variable `kx, ky, n` on the interleaved quantics grid, on one of
two gw-rs topologies: the comb CTTN `cttn` (spine `kx1 - ky1 - n1`, degree
three at `ky1`) or the chain `tt_nblock` (every `n` bit, then `kx` and `ky`
interleaved).

- **G0**: the Dyson map `1 / (mu + 2 a + 2 b + i c)` of three exact rank-2
  inputs `cos kx`, `cos ky`, `omega_n` (gw-rs's TreeACI construction).
- **G0 \* G0**: the product of a patched G0 with itself.
- **G0 \* G0, differently partitioned**: the product of two patched G0 built
  with different caps, so their layouts differ.

Errors are relative L2 errors. For G0 they compare the result with the
analytic G0, since its inputs are exact. For the products they compare the
result with the product of the given input networks (the operation's
contract, which excludes the inputs' own error). "Exact" errors sum over every
grid point (R <= 7). "Sampled" errors (R >= 10) are importance-sampled from
`q = (|f|^2 / S^2 + 1 / V) / 2` with the exact grid norm `S` of the analytic
function, against the analytic G0, and come with their standard error.

The patched runs use the M3 L2 contract (`rtol` relative to `S`) with the
default `ResidualTt` estimator and TreeTCI as its residual engine, the patch
order MSB first, and TreeACI's default `global_tolerance_margin` of 10.
Unpatched TreeACI uses an absolute max-norm tolerance (`tol`) unless stated.

## 3. Extra splits at intermediate tolerances, and the kernel tolerance factor

With the kernel tolerance equal to the driver's per-point allowance `tau`
(`k = 1`), the patch count is not monotone in `rtol`: the measured runs fail
hundreds of times between `rtol = 3e-4` and `1e-3`, and each failure retries
and then splits. A dense audit of every kernel run (exact product of the
restricted inputs, compared with TreeACI's network) shows why. A failing
run is one that converged with an exact RMS error above `tau` or ended at
`IterationLimit`:

- TreeACI's `Converged` does not bound the error by its tolerance: the global
  guard accepts residuals up to `global_tolerance_margin` times the
  tolerance, and its estimate covers the sampled local matrices only. With
  margin 10 almost every failing run at `rtol = 1e-3` is a rank-2 cross whose
  estimate is about half of `tau` while its exact RMS error is about 1.4
  `tau`.
- With margin 10 the failing patches are not on a plateau of singular
  values (largest over the cuts, `N` the patch's grid size). Their spectrum
  has a gap with the second singular value at the threshold:
  `sigma_1 >> sigma_2 ~ tau sqrt(N) >> sigma_3`. The best rank-2 SVD would
  meet the tolerance by a wide margin, but TreeACI chooses the second pivot
  where its residual is already near `tau`. The band of `rtol` with extra
  splits is where many patches have `sigma_2` at this threshold.
- Margin 1 removes the false convergence, but 287 of its 707 runs end at
  `IterationLimit` instead, nearly all at rank 1 or 2.
- Retrying cannot help: the adapter cannot pass the measurement's worst
  points to TreeACI, which takes no pivots.

With `k = 3` the failed measurements of the mixed product drop from 37 to 496
per run to at most 12 (G0: from 1 to 13 to none), the product stays within six
patches of the common refinement (133), and the runs in the band take about
half the time (32.7 to 15.8 s and 44.6 to 18.5 s). The errors fall from 0.37

by -0.3 % to +18 %. `k = 10` overshoots: the mixed product stays at 133
patches but G0 needs more patches than with `k = 3` (24 to 35 against 19 to
33), with errors of 0.05 to 0.09 times `rtol` and 11 to 40 % more parameters
than `k = 1`. As a check, G0 at `k = 10`, `rtol = 1e-3` and at `k = 3`,
`rtol = 3e-4` give the same kernel tolerance and the same network (95442
parameters, error 8.3e-5). These runs used the default of the time, `k = 1`; the
maintainer then set the default to 3 (2026-11-11).

## 5. Tolerance-boundary cycling of unpatched TreeACI (#784)

On `tt_nblock` the maximum rank keeps changing by a few units until the last
sweep at every temperature of section 4, so the per-cut stability window
never closes and every run ends at `MaxSweeps` after 50 sweeps. At T = 0.03
and 0.01 the rank is within 3 % of its final value from sweep 7 or 8, so most
of the time is spent after it has settled: timings of unpatched TreeACI on
this workload measure #784, not the operation.

The fix in progress (`fix/784-rank-oscillation`, strong LU pivot hints)
probed on the same workload, run sequentially on one core (`bin-fix` is
`9dde54ca` with the fix's three commits; see `784probe/PROVENANCE`):

`docs/experiments/treeaci-784-r10-counterexample.json`). Convergence also
depends on the seed there, so every timing comparison needs several seeds.

## Conclusions

- `patched_elementwise` with TreeACI meets its L2 contract on every exact
  check, including differently partitioned inputs (sections 1 and 2).
- With the kernel at the driver's tolerance, TreeACI's convergence criterion
  causes split cascades at intermediate tolerances; an adapter-level
  tolerance factor of about 3 removes them (section 3).
- On the comb at T = 0.1, where the comb is the better topology, patching
  does not pay at errors down to about 1e-4: the patched runs are 3 to 42
  times slower than unpatched TreeACI at matched accuracy. Below 8.5e-5 there
  is no unpatched comparison yet (section 6).
- The workload where patching might pay, gw-rs's `tt_nblock` at T = 0.01
  with rank around 375, cannot be timed until #784 is fixed (section 5).
